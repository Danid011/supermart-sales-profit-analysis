## Project Overview

This project analyses Supermart sales data from 2014 to 2017 to understand sales performance, product profitability, and the impact of discounts on business outcomes.

The project uses SQL and Tableau to transform data into meaningful visualizations and business insights.

## Key Findings

* Technology recorded the highest category sales, at approximately 836,154.
* Furniture sales were approximately 742,000.
* Office Supplies sales were approximately 719,047.
* The no-discount band generated approximately 320,988 in profit.
* The 21–49% discount band recorded a loss of approximately 38,311.
* The 50–80% discount band recorded a loss of approximately 97,065.

These findings suggest that high discount levels may significantly reduce profitability.
